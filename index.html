SPARTAN CODE & HERO’S TOOLKIT

THE GODS SET THE TRIAL. YOU CHOOSE THE BATTLE. BECOME THE WARRIOR.

CHOOSE YOUR LEVEL

GOGGINS I, II, and III are all optional, adjustable intensity levels—not obligations. Maxi chooses according to recovery and existing training.

ADJUST THE TRIAL

Scale duration, intensity, load, or repetitions to suit the day. Use the listed equipment or lower-impact alternatives where appropriate. Training already completed counts when deciding what is sensible.

RESPECT RECOVERY

Warm up for physical missions, make safe adjustments, and stop a movement that feels wrong. Rest, an easier alternative, or a reflection day can be the right choice.
